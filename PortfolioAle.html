Formula Student: Structural & Static Analysis

Under FSG regulations, safety critical structures must withstand high load thresholds. I engineered and validated the vehicle's driver harness restraint system, which according to FSG rules must resist a 20 kN force (approx. 2000 kg) in every loading direction while adhering to strict dimensional limits.

The development process combined CAD geometry definition, static finite element analysis (FEM) in Ansys to locate stress concentrations, and physical validation tests. These engineering methodologies were presented during the Static Events (Design Event, Cost & Manufacturing, Business Plan Presentation).

Bachelor's Degree Thesis

TPMS lattice structure for wheelchair cushions

Design and 3D printing of a lattice structure to reduce contact pressure

My thesis focuses on the optimization of TPMS lattice structures to improve ergonomics and prevent pressure ulcers in wheelchair users. Through structural simulations and mechanical testing on TPU printed samples, I analyze the influence of cell density and thickness on pressure distribution, achieving a more breathable and higher-performing alternative compared to traditional polyurethane foams.
